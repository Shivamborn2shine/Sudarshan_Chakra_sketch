# BLACKTHRONE — White Sketchbook Website PRD

**Version:** 2.0 · **Status:** New creative direction / implementation-ready specification  
**Tagline:** **WE BUILD THE WAY.**  
**Concept:** **Every solution begins as a sketch. We bring it to life.**

> This document supersedes the previous dark, gold, cinematic website direction. The BlackThrone name remains, but the website is **white, playful, pencil-drawn, and interactive**. Product logos and authentic project imagery supply nearly all the saturated color.

## 1. Product vision

Create the flagship portfolio and discovery platform for BlackThrone, a technology and digital solutions company behind OneScan360 and Loopa. The website should feel like stepping into a living designer's sketchbook: loose ideas, doodles, arrows, graph paper, measurements, annotations, outlines and hand-drawn prototypes become polished, functioning digital products as the visitor scrolls.

The emotional narrative is **rough imagination → exploration → construction → tangible solution**. BlackThrone is the place where unstructured challenges gain a clear path forward.

**Desired first impression:** clever, creative, human, experimental, optimistic, competent and memorable. The page should feel alive without sacrificing trust or usability.

**Primary objective:** get qualified business enquiries and demonstrate actual work.  
**Secondary objectives:** drive visits to the official product Instagram accounts; communicate BlackThrone's ecosystem and product maturity; build recognition around “WE BUILD THE WAY.”

## 2. Audience and messaging

- Local business owners seeking customer engagement, retention, ordering, management and digital-presence solutions.
- Founders and teams seeking a technical partner to turn a rough idea into a product.
- Potential collaborators and future recruits exploring the company's work.

**Primary headline:** `EVERY BIG IDEA STARTS SMALL.`  
**Hero resolution:** `WE BUILD THE WAY.`  
**Support copy:** `From a rough thought on paper to technology people actually use. We sketch, experiment, design, and build solutions that help businesses move forward.`

**Microcopy options:** `Got a messy problem? Good.` · `Let's draw a way through.` · `Scribble the problem. We'll build the answer.` · `From what if? to what's next.`

Avoid claims that BlackThrone can literally solve every problem or that in-development products are already available.

## 3. Visual rules — non-negotiable

1. **Overall website stays white or near-white.** No full black sections, dark-mode default, dramatic black-and-gold hero, black background panels, or high-saturation gradient page backgrounds.
2. Paper canvas should be `#FFFFFF` / `#FCFCF9`, with sparse pencil marks in greys `#2D2D2D`, `#747474`, `#C8C8C8`. Very subtle graph-paper lines `#EAEAE6` and occasional ruled paper textures.
3. **Only original logos, screenshots, actual product imagery and real project photography may carry strong colors.** Do not recolor Loopa's vivid purple/lime brand or OneScan360's cyan/pink neon badge. Keep colorful assets in bounded, intentional showcase spaces.
4. All site-native icons, arrows, frames, dividers, decorations, annotations and motion art should look hand-drawn in graphite/ink only—slightly imperfect, varied stroke weights, authentic pencil texture.
5. White-space-heavy modern editorial layout. Avoid turning the site into a childish school notebook; it must remain polished and professional.
6. Sketch motifs can be playful, but every decoration must either explain a flow, guide attention or reinforce the concept. No random clutter over content.
7. Keep the approved BlackThrone Infinity + guiding-spark identity as the recognizable symbol. If the dark source logo is unsuitable against white, obtain an approved black/graphite monochrome treatment instead of inventing a different emblem. The Chakra and Double Ring remain selectable outer treatments.

### Type system

- **Main headings:** bold, clean modern grotesk (e.g. Space Grotesk, Geist, or similar), not script or handwriting everywhere.
- **Handwritten annotations:** a legible hand-drawn font used sparingly for doodle labels, scribbled side-notes, circled words and tiny captions.
- **Body:** highly readable sans serif, 16–18 px; good contrast on white.
- **Wordmark:** BlackThrone's approved identity, or a tasteful approved monochrome adaptation.
- Mix structured production-ready typography with playful secondary marks: a large crisp headline can have a pencil-underlined word or lightly circled phrase.

## 5. Core design metaphor: Sketch → Build → Reality

The homepage plays as a continuous narrative:

1. Visitor sees a white, lightly gridded blank page.
2. An invisible pencil traces a rough question mark and an imperfect arrow/path.
3. This morphs into cleaner graph-paper construction lines and simple wireframe rectangles.
4. Real full-color OneScan360 and Loopa artwork emerges inside the drawings—color enters the experience *only as proof of what was built*.
5. The unfinished line travels onward toward `WE BUILD THE WAY.` and the contact CTA.

The connecting path is a memorable visual motif: imperfect pencil stroke above the fold, measured blueprint line midway, smooth purposeful arrow at the final CTA. Do not let it obscure text or interfere with scrolling.

## 8. Animation and playful interactions

| Interaction | Behavior | Fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero strokes | SVG path draws around 0.8–1.3 s | Static line art immediately |
| Handwritten annotations | Minimal wiggle/drift on pointer or scroll | Stationary text |
| Product cards | Very slow alternating drift; responsive parallax, focus/hover straightens | Stable cards, no auto motion |
| Sketch to reality | Pencil wireframe becomes real image as scroll reaches section | Static image with caption |
| Connecting path | One continuous line traces across selected section boundaries | Static dashed pencil connector |
| Buttons | Border pencil redraw, subtle arrow travel | Color/underline focus state |
| Work gallery | Tape/sketch overlay slightly lifts on hover | Static accessible cards |
| Section reveal | Small opacity/translation and line strokes | Immediate content |

**Rules:** no scroll-jacking, no aggressive tilt, no autoplay sound, no illegible hand-drawn text, no motion that blocks clicks. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. On touch devices, use tap/focus equivalents. Ensure keyboard and screen-reader navigation works. Provide a pause control if ambient animated content becomes distracting or auto-updates.

## 10. Technical architecture

- **Framework:** Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
- **Animation:** GSAP + ScrollTrigger for SVG drawing/scroll choreography, CSS for micro-interactions; optional Motion library for light component transitions. Avoid unnecessary WebGL.
- **Sketch graphics:** original lightweight SVG paths and CSS grids; intentionally irregular strokes with consistent art direction. Use compressed, subtle paper texture only if needed; prioritize pure white.
- **Assets:** supplied original product logos and screenshots; Next/Image or equivalent optimized rendering. Do not programmatically recolor the logos. Keep their colorful areas visually contained.
- **Content:** typed objects for Products, Projects, Services, Site Settings; statuses editable centrally.
- **Lead form:** server-side validation, spam protection, privacy consent and clear feedback; connect to approved mailbox/CRM.
- **SEO:** useful titles/meta, Open Graph cards, sitemap, structured data only when accurate, descriptive alt text.
- **Performance target:** LCP ≤2.5 s, INP ≤200 ms, CLS ≤0.1 at 75th percentile of real-user experience where measurable; aim for strong mobile Lighthouse outcomes.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA target, semantic landmarks, visible focus, contrast, touch target size, no hover-only content, reduced motion.

## 11. Responsive design

**Desktop:** asymmetric editorial grid, two product cards in a designed composition, generous doodle margins, longer path-based scroll transitions.

**Tablet:** reflow product modules into stacked cards, reduce animations, retain margin annotations only where space permits.

**Mobile:** pure white single-column, headline always visible, one product at a time with logo and screenshot, simplified connectors and minimal motion; CTAs at least 44px tall, no horizontal overflow at 320px. Never shrink key text into decorative microtype.

## 13. Acceptance criteria

1. The visual canvas is consistently white/off-white, including header and footer. Colorful areas are limited to authentic supplied brand/product logos, screenshots, and client/project images.
2. The site tells an understandable sketch-to-reality story, without sacrificing readability.
3. Both product showcases link to their exact official Instagram URLs, and use the original assets rather than generated substitutes.
4. BlackThrone's `WE BUILD THE WAY.` statement is prominent and reusable.
5. Product cards move playfully but remain accessible, pausable/static under reduced motion and stable on mobile.
6. All menu items, cards, CTAs and form actions are functional, not placeholder interactions.
7. Product development states and client work are truthful and verifiable.
8. Mobile performance and layout are reviewed at 320px, 375px, 768px, 1024px and desktop widths.
9. Keyboard/focus navigation, reduced motion, alt text, semantic headings and form feedback pass QA.
10. No fake testimonials, stock logos, black-themed sections, or unrelated neon decoration.

**Phase B — Full UI:** sections, pages, product detail templates and actual asset placement.

**Phase C — Interactions:** connecting path, hover/tap states, sketch-to-reality transitions and reduced-motion alternatives.

**Phase D — Functionality:** contact workflow, content data, links, SEO and analytics.

**Phase E — Launch QA:** authentic asset permissions, product status, accessibility, responsive performance and legal copy.
